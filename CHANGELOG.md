## Unreleased (v2-ui)

The UI stage on the `v2-ui` branch: one look for every Story Suite screen (glass panels over SillyTavern's
background, light and dark, the font Nunito). Step 1a: tokens, the theme bridge, the first building blocks.

- **Theme bridge**: light or dark from SillyTavern's body text against its blur tint, re-read when SillyTavern changes its theme; the tint mixed into the glass. New host settings (Settings → Story Suite): **Panel style** Glass (default), Solid (no blur, opaque) or Follow SillyTavern only; **Font** Nunito (default; fetched from Google Fonts only while SillyTavern's "Forbid external media" is off, else a local Nunito or the system font) or the theme's font. The system's reduced transparency turns Glass into Solid; reduced motion turns transitions off.
- **Building blocks** (`src/v2/view/glass.css`, loaded with host.css): glass card `.ss-glass` / `.ss-glass-strong`, section label `.ss-label`, pill `.ss-pill` (soft, outline), progress bar `.ss-bar` (HP/MP/SP/EXP colours), tile `.ss-tile` (icon, label, number badge, tick, lock, chosen), status badge `.ss-status` (icon and time left), ring tones `.ss-tone-*`. As widgets for templates: `ss.tile`, `ss.bar`, `ss.status` ("2t", "40m", "∞", spoken in words), `ss.pill` (WIDGETS.md).
- **UI sample** for developers: Settings → Diagnostics → Show the UI sample, every block in a light and a dark pane over SillyTavern's background (`src/v2/screens/ui-sample.js`).
- Docs: docs/dev/ui-and-screens.md "The look", WIDGETS.md. Tests: `tests/v2/view/theme.test.js`, `tests/v2/ux/glass-css.test.js`, `tests/v2/ux/look-settings.test.js`, `tests/v2/view/widgets/glass.test.js`, `tests/v2/screens/ui-sample.test.js`.

## 2.0.0-beta.1 (`v2` branch)

Story Suite v2, first beta. What's new, known issues and every module's version: [docs/v2/RELEASES.md](docs/v2/RELEASES.md). The player guide: [docs/v2/GUIDE.md](docs/v2/GUIDE.md).

- The version is 2.0.0-beta.1. It sorts after every 2.0.0 alpha and before 2.0.0, so a module that asks for `storyBlock: '>=2.0.0-beta.1'` refuses an alpha host and accepts this one and later ones.
- In this beta, from Story Block: Features switches (F100); Inventory with Equip on…, loot to pick and encumbered; key items and one story-money budget per reply; coins (copper, silver, gold) and prices by place; Choices and Inner chatter; change lines read when written in the story itself; a settings screen that keeps its folds, with Prompt size, Share of the context and Diagnostics; `sdk.callModel` and the `profile` field for modules (Story Director's arc planning needs them); save points, the state inspector and Versions; smaller chat files and safer saving (late-save guard, save on hide). Each with its details below.
- **Weather by rule, and no money in a coin the chat does not know** (beta live test 5: "weather is not working"; a reward of "40 silver" shown as "40 pennies"; MODULES §F122). New command `scene.weather({ text?, key? })` (feature `story-block.weather`; `system` or `ui`, refused for the story): a module's rule sets the weather marked with a key (Story Director: the story day and region, as an `afterCapture` step), or marks the story's own `~ weather` with a key, its words kept; `scene.weather` is `{ text, rev, key? }` and a story line writes no key. A pack may give a `weather` table by season and region (PACKS.md "Weather", `BAD_WEATHER`, query `packs.weather`). Money: in a chat without coins a coin line in a coin word that is not the chat's own (`+ coin 40 silver` where money is pennies) is refused with a Problem naming the chat's money, never paid as 40 pennies (money.js `foreignCoin`); "gp" counts as gold in a gold chat. Tests: `tests/v2/domains/weather-rule.test.js`.
- **Assist safety net for change lines the reply missed** (MODULES §F123). With `story-block.extractor` on (off by default), one cheap call after every reply (its own profile, `assistExtractorProfile`) proposes only the lines the story showed but the block left out: time, place, who is here, weather, quest progress or a turn-in, wounds, an item handed over (setting `assistExtractorKinds`). Never coin, new items, XP, loot or heals. A line the block already has is dropped (C-1 matching). Applied as story lines after the reply's own, under the same rules and caps, receipts `assist: 'now'`. They are marked "(assist)" in Problems and on the changes card, and stored in the hidden comment (`<Changes-assist>`), so Re-read and replay never call the model again. A send waits for it at most 1.5 s; a later answer is stored as `<Changes-assist-next>` and applied on the next reply, marked "(assist, from the previous reply)", with no coin at all. Tests: `tests/v2/assist/extractor.test.js`, `tests/v2/assist/safety-net.test.js`.
- **One queue per connection for model calls** (MODULES §F124). Calls on the same connection profile from sdk.callModel and Assist run one at a time, in order; each keeps its own time limit and signal, and a call that times out or is cancelled while waiting is never made. Different profiles still run side by side; the chat's connection queues after the turn's idle wait. Host setting `callQueue` "Run extra calls one at a time per connection" (default on). Diagnostics has a "Model calls" line with the queue length and waits. Tests: `tests/v2/model-call.test.js` (queue).
- **Modules' own lines on "Changed this reply"** (beta.1 live test: "there's no way for us to know the rolls?"; MODULES §F125). A module's `hooks.replyCard({ index, state, before, changes })` answers that reply's lines (`[{ text, summary?, flag?, note?, icon? }]`); the card draws them as plain text in its list and tells each by its `summary` in the folded header line, and says "Nothing changed" only when no module had a line. RPG Battle uses it for the reply's checks. SPEC §6, GUIDE "Cards". Tests: `tests/v2/screens/reply-card-lines.test.js`, `tests/v2/ux/ux.test.js`.
- **GUIDE "Checks and modifiers"**: RPG Battle's check table is the default; the Assist pre-roll is the optional strict mode (it fixes stat and difficulty from the reader's message for one cheap call).
- **`ctx.gather(name, args)`: every other module's answer to a hook** (MODULES §F126). A command asks each other module's `hooks[name]({ state, query }, args)` on the state it sees, null-safe; RPG Battle asks `checkModifiers` so other modules can adjust a check (bounded there). SPEC §3.2, §6. Tests: `tests/v2/gather.test.js`.
- **Modules' own lines on "Changed this reply"** (beta.1 live test: "there's no way for us to know the rolls?"; MODULES §F123). A module's `hooks.replyCard({ index, state, before, changes })` answers that reply's lines (`[{ text, summary?, flag?, note?, icon? }]`); the card draws them as plain text in its list and tells each by its `summary` in the folded header line, and says "Nothing changed" only when no module had a line. RPG Battle uses it for the reply's checks. SPEC §6, GUIDE "Cards". Tests: `tests/v2/screens/reply-card-lines.test.js`, `tests/v2/ux/ux.test.js`.
- **Use on… in the Inventory** (beta.1 live test: "use item is missing"). A consumable's row has **Use on…** with the members a module answering `items.useOn` names (RPG Battle: whom a dose would help now, nobody while a fight has the party); a choice runs `item.use { stack, target }`. Bag management like Equip on… and Give… (rule 6; MODULES §F96 amended). `inventory.equipment` gives `users: { [stack]: [{ id, name }] }` (only consumables the party holds, only members; `{}` without such a module, so Story Block alone draws no picker). SPEC §7, GUIDE "Inventory". Tests: `tests/v2/screens/use-on.test.js`.
- **A step after the reply's last story line: `afterCapture`** (beta-candidate live test: a story turn-in sat at "checked where this reply ends up, before the next reply" and read as not done; MODULES §F121, SPEC §4.2 step 3). A module's `afterCapture(view, { index, type })` hook runs once right after a reply's capture, after all its story lines, and its `{ commands }` run as `system` steps on the scene the reply ended up in, stored on that reply right after its story receipts (receipt `afterCapture: true`, with `replan`). Not run for an empty capture, a click or a system step. A Continue runs it again after its own lines; a Re-read asks the hook again after the new lines at the old steps' seeds; swipe and delete take the steps back with the reply. `bus.run` takes `reply` for a system step kept on a reply that is not the head (also given to a Re-read's replan steps), `makeView` takes `state` and `reply`, `store.replaceStory` takes `settled`. Story Director settles its waiting turn-ins there. Tests: `tests/v2/after-capture.test.js`.
- **Events is under Diagnostics** (live test: "the information, shouldn't those be under diagnostic?"). The Events panel (every pack event with its condition, status and time) is debug information that tells what is coming, so it moved from Story Block's fold to a sub-fold of **Settings → Diagnostics**, folded until opened, with the note "For checking packs. It shows upcoming events and their conditions, so it can spoil the story." New for modules: `settings.place: 'diagnostics'` (and `place` on a `settings.folds` item) draws that fold there; another value warns and is ignored. With no report there is still a Diagnostics fold for them. SPEC §6; host-ui.js `EVENTS_FOLD`, `diagnosticsKeys`, `DIAGNOSTICS_TEXT.checksHint`; registry.js `SETTINGS_PLACES`; events `EVENTS_NOTE`.
- **Inner chatter is no longer drawn under replies** (beta-candidate live test: "inline inner chatter should be removed"; MODULES §F115). Each thought shows next to the person who had it: on Story Director's World → Now (`chatter.head`, unchanged), or, without Story Director (or with its World view off), on Story Block's Scene screen under each person present, the name then the thought (new read-only query `scene.chatter`). The Choices buttons under the latest reply, the capture, the feature, its grammar line (the static prompt is byte-identical) and the `chatterInPrompt` tail line are as they were.
- **Weather is a scene line** (beta-candidate live test: weather on the reply's Status card; MODULES §F120). New change line `~ weather cold drizzle, low fog`, kept as `scene.weather = { text, rev }` (flattened, no markup, at most 60 code points; the same text again changes nothing); read-only query `scene.weather` → `{ text } | null`, which Story Director's Status card reads. Descriptive only: no mechanic reads it. Feature `story-block.weather` (Weather, on): its grammar line ("only when the weather changes, not every reply") is in the static part only while it is on, so the fixed prompt changes once when it is switched (and once on updating, as it is on). Shown on Scene and Home's scene panel; the tail's Place line ends with it, cut to 26 code points (about 8 tokens). A `scratch` field of the scene, so Re-read, swipes, a middle delete and a reload give what a rebuild gives; added to both QA fuzz generators.
- **The people here are on the World screen; Cast is Dossiers, last** (beta live test: "Cast becomes redundant when present is already present character in world tab"; MODULES §F119). The default tab order puts NPC State Delta's tab and screen (`npc-state-delta/cast`, now titled Dossiers) last, after the Director (`DEFAULT_ORDER.cast` 210); a saved order keeps its place. New read-only query `chatter.head` → the newest reply's Inner chatter as `[{ name, text }]`, read as the strip under the reply reads it (`null` while Inner chatter is off), so Story Director's World screen can show each person's thought. **Scene starts hidden while Story Director's World is installed** ("is Scene doing the same thing as World?": World → Now has the place, kind, region, "moved this reply", the story time and the people): it stays listed in Settings → Story Suite → Tabs to show again; the reader's choice wins (new `storySuite.tabs.shown` for one shown again; a saved `hidden` hides it; an order saved before that names Scene keeps it shown). Without Story Director, Scene shows as before; Home's scene panel is unchanged. New read-only query `scene.moved` → whether the head reply moved the scene to another place (from its receipts, so swipes and deletes follow), for World's "moved this reply" mark.
- **Final-break fixes** (the beta candidate's last break: FB-1, FB-2 medium, the story could make value; FB-3 low).
  *A story line and the Continue's block that lists it are one change* (FB-1): C-1 read an echoed line once only within one read, so a reply cut off before or inside its block (`+ coin 12` in the story, then a Continue's `<Changes>\n+ coin 12\n</Changes>`; or `+ item Rope` in the story and the Continue finishing the block with `+ item Rope`) paid twice, live and on Re-read. The lines a read takes from the story are now kept as the story's (`<Changes story>` in the hidden comment), and a Continue's block line that repeats one of them is its echo (`<Changes-echo>`), never read: one block line per story line, and a Continue's story line that repeats an earlier block line is read once. Re-read equals live, and a second Re-read changes nothing. A comment's blocks are read only up to its first `<Changes-…>` section, so a kept body with a second open tag never reads them (capture.js; SPEC §4.4). *Loot is the story's own* (FB-2): what the reader takes from a `? loot` line (`inventory.lootTake`) is marked `story: true` as a `+ item` line's is, so a pack's goods taken from the story's loot sell as the story's (RPG Battle: at most their worth at the sell rate), not past the reply's money budget; `inventory.grant` stays unmarked (no story line reaches it). *Bare money words* (FB-3): `+ item Coins | qty: 5`, `+ item Coin` and `+ item 5 coins` are money in the smallest unit, as `+ item Gold Coins` already was without Coins, and `+ coin 5 coins` with Coins pays 5 of the smallest coin (money.js `coinValue`; MODULES §F108).

## 2.0.0-alpha.1 (in progress, `v2` branch)

Story Suite v2: a fresh start. State is JSON stored in each reply, every action is a command with a receipt, modules register through an SDK, screens are templates/layouts with widgets. Built so far: the state store, command bus, registry and turn pipeline, core scene and inventory (one currency), packs, events, layouts, mounts, widgets and the fishing example. Status: [docs/v2/STATUS.md](docs/v2/STATUS.md).

- **Beta-candidate break fixes (RPG Battle's a1/a2 findings, Story Block's parts).** *The story's own goods* (B-a2-2): every stack a `+ item` line gives is marked `story: true` (also on `inventory.items` rows) whatever its def, so a pack item the story names (counted in the reply's goods budget by worth) sells as the story's own does, never above the reply's money budget at a capital; the mark goes with a split, a hand-over and a merge (a stack it joins is the story's too). *A reader's ladder never raises money* (B-a2-1): a reader's rank pays the reward of the first pack's rank at its first level (the last one that starts at or below it), not the one at its place, so twelve one-level ranks pay at level 12 what the pack pays at level 12. *Re-read plans a cue again* (B-a1-13): a system step may return `replan: true` (its receipt keeps `replan: { payload }`); a Re-read of the reply it is kept on runs it again on the new story changes (at its place, its seed) instead of applying its old ops, so RPG Battle's story-time heal follows the reply's clock as it now reads (time edited out heals nothing). SPEC §3, §4.2, §7; PACKS.md ranks.
- **Change lines in the story are taken more narrowly, and Re-read reads what the live reply read** (beta-candidate break a1, confirm soak C-1, C-3). A line written in the story is taken only when it parses completely (no quote marks, no sentence end, no ", she said" after the head, no `<Changes>` tag in it), stands in a paragraph of change lines only, is not inside a quotation left open above it, and is not in a code block, a `>` quote, `<details>`/`<style>`/`<pre>` or a multi-line comment: "+ coin 5, she said, and not one more." and a fenced example of the format stay story (with a `STRAY` Problem) and pay nothing. "– coin 5", "− coin 5", "＋ coin 5" and "+ coins 5" are told (`STRAY`). A story line the block repeats is read once. The lines a read leaves in the story are listed in the hidden comment, so a Re-read leaves them too after a stop, a Continue or a tag on the line. The hidden comment keeps `--` exactly (word joiner, undone on read), so Re-read no longer turns "Rope -- frayed" into "Rope"; old comments read as before. A close tag inside a line (`| who: </Changes>`) no longer ends the block and shows its rest. An open block's last line with nothing after it is never applied (it may be "+ item Ro" cut by the length limit): it is a `CUT_OFF` Problem, and the Continue that finishes it reads the whole line once. Choices or chatter told "not used" while their feature was off stay unshown when it is turned on, until a Re-read. New in capture.js: `markOff`, `hasHidden`; `cutLine`'s `stopped` is gone (SPEC §4.4).
- **Money words agree** (a1). With Coins, `+ item Silver Coins`, `+ item 3 silver coins`, `+ item Silvers` and `? loot 2 gold pieces` are money in the smallest unit, as `+ coin 3 silver coins` is; the currency's name that is not one of the coins (`+ coin 2 gold` with copper and silver) is refused with the coins named, not paid as copper (MODULES §F108). money.js: `countedMoney`.
- **Unload saving measures bytes** (a3). The 64 KiB keepalive limit is in UTF-8 bytes, so a Japanese or emoji-heavy chat is no longer sent with keepalive (refused by the browser) and the page asks before closing (SPEC §3.3). chat-file.js: `utf8Bytes`.
- **Diagnostics shares no chat text** (a3). Problems are listed by code, module and reply only (their messages named items and people), quoted text is cut from the first quote mark to the last (a lone one takes the rest of the line), and more key shapes are removed: short `sk-`/`pk-` keys, AWS key ids, JWTs and long runs of letters and digits.
- **Pack calendar holidays.** A pack's `calendar` may name `holidays: [{ name, month, day }]` (at most 60; month by name or number, kept as the name; day within that month; names flattened, invisible characters out, cut to 60; anything else is `BAD_CALENDAR`), and `packs.calendar()` gives them as `holidays` (left out when there are none, so other calendars read as before). Story Director's World screen shows them under Upcoming. The Ersveil pack adds none (its lore has no dated holidays). Tests: `tests/v2/packs/ersveil-format.test.js`, and `tests/v2/packs/holidays-world.test.js` with a Story Director checkout beside this one (skipped without).
- **Your order of tabs** (live test: "can the tabs be re-arranged?", "the main tabs as well"). **Settings → Story Suite → Tabs** lists every tab and screen of every installed module once (a module tab and its screen are one row: NPC State Delta's Cast tab and Cast screen go by `npc-state-delta/cast`), with where it shows, **Up** / **Down** (real buttons; Enter moves one place, not two: SillyTavern's page-wide Enter handler no longer clicks them again), **Shown**, and **Reset to default**. One saved list, `extension_settings.storySuite.tabs = { order: [id…], hidden: [id…] }`, orders both the Story panel's tab strip and the game view's navigation. Ids the list does not name (a module installed since) go at the end in default order; ids of a module not installed are passed over and keep their slot. Hidden ones leave the strip and the navigation but stay in Settings, and a screen opened by name (a fight starting) still opens; Home is always first and always shown. New default order: Home, Party, Inventory, Quests, World, Map, Fight, Crafting, Trade, Cast, Scene, Chronicle, then other modules' screens, Director last (`screens.js` `DEFAULT_ORDER`, `SUITE_NAV`: the suite's own keep their place whatever `order` they say, so Cast's old 35 no longer puts it before Inventory). On a phone the tab strip and the game view's bar scroll the chosen one into view (`scroll-cue.js` `bringIntoView`). New: `src/v2/view/tab-order.js` (`orderNav`, `visibleNav`, `moveNav`, `setNavHidden`, `navKey`, `createTabOrderStore`), `host-ui.js` `TABS_FOLD`, `TABS_TEXT`, the screen mount's optional `inNav(id)`, and `mountUi(…).tabs`.
- **Text boxes follow the theme** (live test: "the search text box is glaring"). Since SillyTavern only styles its own `.text_pole`, selects, textareas, checkboxes and sliders, a bare text, search or number box in a screen (the Journal and Days search, Chronicle's edit fields, a probe or pack screen) drew in the browser's bright white on a dark theme. Checked on a dark and a light theme. The rules weigh no more than a bare element and sit under `:where()` roots, so SillyTavern's own UI is untouched and a module's own field classes still win (none of the module repos needed a change).
- **Extension panels one level up, the assist's profile as a dropdown, and Diagnostics** (live test #4: "NPC State Delta's settings are a sub of a sub fold"; "Story Block doesn't have the dropdown"; "diagnostics missing"). A module's own panel (`settings.section` / `render`) is drawn right in its fold under the features line, its schema groups below as sub-folds: NPC State Delta's whole settings (its drawer held open, its groups the second level) and Story Block's **Assist**, never three folds deep, at 1440 and 390 px. **Assist → Connection profile for these calls** is now a `profile` field (the dropdown of #125: *Same as chat*, then Connection Manager's profiles by name, a plain note with none or when the picked one is gone) in Story Block's **Assist** group, and the pre-roll and extractor calls use it; a name typed before is the same value. The v2 host only loads `host.css`, so v1's rule that held a moved drawer open had gone: NPC State Delta's controls were collapsed under its own header. New **Settings → Diagnostics** fold (last before Versions): **Copy report** / **Save report** of a plain report (versions, features, each module's settings with keys, tokens, passwords and addresses left out, the prompt size and context size, the last 20 Problems, saving, the chat's counts and size, Story Suite console errors caught, and each module's optional `hooks.diagnostics()` section), never chat text. MODULES §F116–F118, `src/v2/diagnostics.js`.
- **A module's model call never mixes with your reply.** A call on the chat's connection made while your reply is being built or awaited now waits until the reply is in (a named connection profile still goes at once), and Story Block marks its own calls, the assist helpers' too, so their prompt is never taken for the reply's and never gets Story Suite's notes (MODULES §G).
- **Model calls for modules** (MODULES §G). `sdk.callModel({ profile, system, prompt, maxTokens, signal, feature })` → `{ ok, text, error }`: a module's own call to a model outside the story (Story Director's arc planner, RPG Battle's monster flavour). On a Connection Manager profile it goes straight to that profile's API with its own settings, so the reader's connection is never switched; `'chat'` or empty uses the chat's connection. It never posts to the chat or touches its history, never throws (`FEATURE_OFF`, `BUSY`, `TIMEOUT`, `ABORTED`, `NO_PROFILE`, `EMPTY`, `FAILED`… with a plain message), allows one call in flight per module, stops after 60 s (`timeoutMs`) or on the caller's `signal`, and logs only the module, the profile's name, the time and the outcome. New settings field type **`profile`**: a dropdown of the Connection Manager's profiles plus "Same as chat", storing the name, with a plain note when there are none or the Connection Manager is off. Testkit: `createFakeModel(script)`, a scripted model for `createHost({ callModel })`.
- **Choices and Inner chatter are back** (v1's, in a v2 form; MODULES §F115). Two features, both off: **Choices** (`story-block.choices`) asks the story for 2–4 short next moves as `? choice Climb the wall` lines; the latest reply shows them as buttons, and a click only puts the text in the message box (it never sends). **Inner chatter** (`story-block.chatter`) asks for up to 3 unspoken NPC thoughts as `~ chatter Minke: …he'd better not die down there`, shown in a folded strip under each reply (the name opens NPC State Delta's dossier when it knows them) and kept out of later prompts unless the new setting *Send the last reply's thoughts back to the story* is on. Both are **display kinds** (new: a capture kind's `display: { perReply, noun, read }`): no command runs and nothing is stored; they are read again from the reply's hidden block when drawn, so Re-read, swipes, deletes and a reload agree. Over the cap (4 choices, 3 thoughts) or unreadable lines are Problems on the card; cut by code points; drawn as text only.
- **Show what's sent** (back from v1). Under the prompt size in the Story Suite fold, a fold with the exact text of the next reply's Story Suite part: the instructions, this reply's part and what an extension sends on its own, per part with its extension and size (and the lines left out to fit), and **Copy** for all of it. NPC State Delta sends its dossiers (often 2,000–4,000 tokens) under its own prompt keys, so the prompt size and the share of the context left them out. A module now says what it sends through a new hook, `ownPrompt(view)` → `[{ label, text }]` (SPEC §5, §6); the prompt size shows it as a row of its own ("NPC State Delta, sent by itself") and in the total, and it counts toward the share: in a small context the notes for each reply get what the instructions and that text leave (never below 200 tokens). The instructions are unchanged and never cut. `turn.previewFit()` now gives `{ parts, fit, own }`; `contextFit` and `assemble` take the modules' own tokens (`fit.own`).
- **A change line written in the story is read** (live test: no fight could start). The model wrote `! encounter 8 Weir Rat — cellar pack | tier: weak | stakes: normal` in the story, on its own line just before `<Changes>`, and only lines inside the block were read: no offer, the line left in the shown text, and no word why. - **Coins** (live test: "currency is pennies, but the story says copper and silver": the lorebook and card name three coins, and Story Suite knew one name).
